What We Offer

A complete spectrum
of allied health care

From acute injury management to long-term neurological rehabilitation — our multidisciplinary team has the depth to treat any condition.

Physiotherapy
Our flagship service — hands-on treatment combining joint mobilisation, soft tissue work, dry needling, and personalised rehabilitation programs. Same-day bookings available.
Learn more →
Clinical Pilates & Group Exercise
Structured exercise classes led by qualified physiotherapists. Increase strength, improve range of motion, and lower injury risk — for all fitness levels.
Learn more →
GLA:D Program
The internationally-validated Gold Standard program for hip and knee osteoarthritis. Small groups (≈6), screened and tracked from start to finish.
Learn more →
Lipoedema & Lymphoedema
Specialist physiotherapy for complex lymphatic conditions, delivered by therapists trained in advanced compression and drainage techniques.
Learn more →
Podiatry
Assessment and treatment of foot, ankle, and lower limb conditions. Custom orthotics, biomechanical analysis, and sports podiatry available.
Learn more →
Dietitian
Nutritional support integrated with your physiotherapy care. Evidence-based dietary planning for weight management, injury recovery, and chronic conditions.
Learn more →
Remedial Massage
Targeted therapeutic massage for muscle tension, sports recovery, and chronic pain. Delivered by qualified therapists as standalone or complementary care.
Learn more →
Hydrotherapy
A therapeutic 30°C pool with underwater treadmill and resistance jets. Buoyancy reduces joint load while physiotherapists guide targeted recovery programs.
Learn more →
RecoverX Centre
A dedicated recovery and rehabilitation hub blending professional sports technology with physiotherapy-guided programming for accelerated recovery.
Learn more →
