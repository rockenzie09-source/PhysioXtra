Who We Are

Elite care for everyone

PhysioXtra was founded in 1996 by Kath Chigwidden with one clear purpose — to make the kind of physiotherapy previously reserved for elite athletes and private hospitals accessible to every South Australian, regardless of their circumstances.

That vision has guided every decision since: which services to offer, which technology to invest in, how to train our team, and where to open our clinics. Today, with six locations and twelve specialist services, we remain as committed to that original purpose as ever.

Accredited & registered
All practitioners are registered with AHPRA and their relevant professional bodies.
Multidisciplinary team
Physios, podiatrists, dietitians, and massage therapists working together around your care.

Our Journey

Three decades of
making a difference

From a single clinic to a network of six — every step forward has been guided by our commitment to accessible, excellent care.

1996
PhysioXtra founded
Kath Chigwidden opens the first clinic in Marion with a mission to bring elite physiotherapy to every South Australian.
2002
Norwood clinic opens
Expansion to the eastern suburbs brings PhysioXtra to a new community, with additional physiotherapy and clinical Pilates services.
2008
Hydrotherapy facility launched
State-of-the-art hydrotherapy pool installed at Marion — one of the first private physiotherapy practices in SA to offer this service.
2014
Lokomat arrives in SA
PhysioXtra becomes one of the first private clinics in South Australia to offer Lokomat robotic gait training to the public.
2018
CBD & south coast expansion
New clinics open in Pirie Street (CBD), Seaford, and Victor Harbor, bringing comprehensive care to the south coast.
2023
RecoverX & ArmeoSpring
RecoverX Recovery Centre launches alongside the ArmeoSpring upper limb rehab system at Norwood — setting a new benchmark in private physiotherapy technology.
