Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about physiotherapy and our services.

No, you do not need a GP referral to see a physiotherapist in Australia. You can book directly with us at any time. However, if you have a chronic condition and wish to access Medicare rebates through a Chronic Disease Management (CDM) plan, your GP will need to provide a referral and a care plan.

Initial assessments are usually 45–60 minutes, allowing us to fully understand your condition and develop a personalised treatment plan. Follow-up appointments are typically 30–45 minutes. Your physiotherapist will advise on the right appointment length for your specific needs.

Please bring any relevant medical imaging (X-rays, MRI scans), referral letters or care plans, your Medicare card, health insurance card, and any WorkCover or NDIS documentation if applicable. Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows access to the area being treated.

Yes, we accept all major private health funds. We have HICAPS facilities in all our clinics, so you can claim your rebate on the spot — you only pay the gap. The amount covered depends on your level of cover, so we recommend checking with your fund before your appointment.

Yes, we treat patients under WorkCover SA, motor vehicle accident claims, DVA (Department of Veterans' Affairs), and NDIS plans. Please contact us before your first appointment so we can arrange the appropriate paperwork and ensure a smooth claims process.

The number of sessions varies greatly depending on the nature and severity of your condition, your overall health, and how well you respond to treatment. Acute injuries may resolve in 3–6 sessions, while chronic conditions or post-surgical rehabilitation can require ongoing treatment over several months. Your physiotherapist will give you a realistic treatment timeline at your initial assessment.

Yes! Our online booking system allows you to select any of our six clinics across metropolitan Adelaide. You can book an initial assessment or a follow-up appointment at a time that suits you. If you need help choosing the right clinic or service, don't hesitate to call us on (08) 8297 1111.

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. This allows us to offer the time to another patient who may be waiting. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee. If you're running late on the day, please call the clinic as soon as possible.
